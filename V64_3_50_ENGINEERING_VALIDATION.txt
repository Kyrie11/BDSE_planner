Root cause
----------
The remaining V50 check incorrectly treated candidate action integers as global
proposal identities across different planner states.  They are not.  The BDSE
candidate bank is regenerated every tick and `_prune_candidate_pool()` performs
state-dependent pruning/reordering before exposing final 0..K-1 action slots;
metadata records `pool_original_index` for exactly this reason.

This is not evidence of RSMR/candidate-generator drift.  Checked against the V49
source manifest in this package:
  bdse/planner/candidate_generator.py : exact V49 SHA match
  bdse/planner/tournament.py          : exact V49 SHA match
The only expected V49 historical-manifest mismatch in the V50 tree is
`bdse/planner/nuplan_planner.py`, which V50 intentionally extends with probe
instrumentation.

Repair
------
- Freeze the 502 V49 scene cohort/folds and frozen RSMR selector/config, not a
  cross-state candidate slot integer.
- CONTROL/TREATMENT must still have identical pre-intervention action/time traces.
- Their first live RSMR proposal iteration/time, live proposal slot and incumbent
  slot must match at the same state.
- The planner records an instrumentation-only SHA256 fingerprint of the live
  proposal trajectory (1e-4 quantized) plus maneuver semantics.  Fingerprint,
  maneuver id, pool-original index, maneuver label and theta must match C/T.
- Live Q/P/E must match C/T at that same pre-intervention event.
- CONTROL preserves incumbent; TREATMENT executes the exact live winner once;
  later proposals preserve incumbent.
- V49 offline full_selected_action is retained as `offline_v49_action_slot`
  provenance plus `live_vs_offline_action_slot_equal`; it is not a gate.
- SIOR fitting consumes live event-aligned Q/P/E and paired closed-loop labels.
